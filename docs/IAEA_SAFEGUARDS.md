## IAEA Regulatory, Non-Proliferation & Safety Compliance

The Model 1 Acoustic Inertial Confinement Fusion (AICF) reactor architecture is engineered from the ground up to comply with International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) standards, statutory non-proliferation protocols, and nuclear safety guidelines:

### 1. Non-Proliferation & Safeguards (IAEA INFCIRC/153)
* **Proliferation-Resistant Fuel Cycle:** Operates strictly on a closed-loop Deuterium-Tritium (D-T) fuel cycle with on-site $Pb_{83}Li_{17}$ tritium breeding. It requires no enriched fissile materials (e.g., Uranium or Plutonium), completely eliminating proliferation risks.
* **Tritium Inventory Control:** Total active tritium inventory is kept under $< 1.5\text{ grams}$ inside the active loop at any time, staying below IAEA material control thresholds.

### 2. Operational Safety & Passive Shutdown
* **Sub-Critical Drive Mechanics:** Fusion ignition relies continuously on active 3D acoustic wave convergence. Removing driver actuation immediately stops fusion reactions within milliseconds, rendering catastrophic runaways physically impossible.
* **Low Activation Materials:** The non-magnetic Austenitic ODS High-Entropy Superalloy vessel minimizes long-term structural neutron activation. Decommissioning waste qualifies for Class A/B low-level radiological disposal within 50 years.

### 3. Containment & Environmental Safeguards
* **Double-Walled Secondary Containment:** The liquid $Pb_{83}Li_{17}$ primary loop operates inside an evacuated, inert-argon containment boundary to prevent tritium migration and liquid-metal oxidation.
